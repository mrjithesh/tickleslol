Why laughter matters

Funny isn't frivolous

Laughter is one of the most accessible tools for mental wellness we have — and one of the most underused. Tickles.LOL makes it a daily habit without it feeling like a chore.

For comedians, the act of crafting and sharing jokes is itself therapeutic — a creative outlet, a source of community connection, and a way to process the world through humour.

🧠
Laughter reduces cortisol The body's stress hormone drops measurably after sustained laughter
💪
Boosts immune function Regular laughter is linked to increased immune cell production
🤝
Builds social bonds Shared humour is one of the fastest ways to feel human connection

About us

Built for laughs.
Backed by science.

Tickles.LOL started from a simple observation: the world has a lot of aspiring comedians who need honest feedback, and a lot of people who desperately need to laugh more. Neither group had a good home.

We built the bridge. A community where the act of getting better at comedy and the act of improving your mental health are the same thing.

Our mission

To make laughter as normal a part of daily wellness as exercise or sleep — and to give every aspiring comedian, regardless of background or access, a real shot at getting better at their craft through honest, kind, community-driven feedback.

🛡️

Creators own their work

Every joke submitted is timestamped and owned entirely by its author. We hold a limited display licence — nothing more.

🤝

Kindness is a rule

Feedback must be constructive. Our community guidelines exist to protect comedians from cruelty dressed up as critique.

🧠

Laughter is medicine

We take the mental health dimension seriously. The platform is designed around habits that actually improve wellbeing.

🌱

Amateur is honourable

We celebrate the person trying their first joke as much as the seasoned open-mic regular. Everyone starts somewhere.
