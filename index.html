Why laughter matters

Funny isn't frivolous

Laughter is one of the most accessible tools for mental wellness we have — and one of the most underused. Tickles.LOL makes it a daily habit without it feeling like a chore.

For comedians, the act of crafting and sharing jokes is itself therapeutic — a creative outlet, a source of community connection, and a way to process the world through humour.

🧠
Laughter reduces cortisol The body's stress hormone drops measurably after sustained laughter
💪
Boosts immune function Regular laughter is linked to increased immune cell production
🤝
Builds social bonds Shared humour is one of the fastest ways to feel human connection
"I posted my first joke on a Tuesday night when I was feeling really low. By Thursday I had 60 ratings and someone wrote 'this made my commute'. That small thing mattered so much." — Marcus D., Tickles.LOL member since 2024
